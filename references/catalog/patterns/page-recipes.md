# pattern.page-recipes.v1

A page recipe is an ordered starting composition of registered block families
and variants. Applying a recipe creates ordinary structured blocks with fresh
stable IDs. The recipe has no continuing ownership of the page: every block
can subsequently be edited, reordered, removed, or have its variant changed.

Recipes provide starter structure and fixture-quality content only. They do not
copy licensed demonstration content or bypass the page draft/publish workflow.
