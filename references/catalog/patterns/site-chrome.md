# pattern.site-chrome.v1

Headers, flyout menus, banners, and footers are persistent site chrome rather
than page blocks. Their variants are configured in the revisioned site-settings
draft and rendered from the published settings on every public page.

Navigation content remains semantic and ordered. Header and flyout variants
may change grouping, descriptions, icon treatment, mobile presentation, and
call-to-action placement, but they may not duplicate navigation data. Banners
are optional, have accessible message and action content, and may be marked
dismissible only when their meaning is not essential.
