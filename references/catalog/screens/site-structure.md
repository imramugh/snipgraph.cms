# screen.site-structure.v1

Site structure is the page-aware map of the website. Its summary distinguishes
published pages, draft work, broken internal targets, and pages with no inbound
link. The navigation snapshot links directly to the revisioned navigation editor.

Each page card shows its public path, publication state, ordered governed block
layout, navigation membership, and inbound-link count. Editors can open the page
layout editor or public preview from the card. A link-health panel identifies the
source and target of every unresolved internal link; valid external, email, and
telephone links are excluded from internal health checks.

The screen is diagnostic and navigational. Mutations continue through the shared
page and settings services, preserving draft, publishing, authorization, revision,
and audit behavior.
