# screen.content-editor.v1

Use for editing one versioned content item. On wide viewports, use the governed
three-pane admin workspace: block outline on the left, selected-block form in
the flexible center, and a sticky publication and history inspector on the
right. On narrower viewports, the outline and inspector open as accessible
drawers while the form retains the canvas width. The compact page header
identifies the item, current draft state, save status, preview action, and
publish action. Field errors appear adjacent to their inputs and in a navigable
summary.

Saving creates a draft revision. Publishing is always explicit and never an
implicit consequence of saving.
